Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 29, 2026 · Last updated: July 8, 2026
The short version: Remember Project is built to protect your family's most private memories. We store only what's necessary to preserve your recordings and make them accessible to your family. We never sell your data. Your recordings are visible only to members of your family vault unless a vault admin deliberately shares an individual recording using the Share Recording feature (see Section 7A). Your recordings are never analyzed or transcribed, are never used to train AI models, and are never used to identify anyone by their voice. Your data is yours — always exportable, always deletable.

1. Who we are

Remember Project ("we," "us," or "our") is an iOS application developed and operated by Second Chapter Technologies LLC, based in Texas, United States. You can reach us at support@rememberproject.app.

2. What information we collect

  • Account information: Email address and display name, used for authentication and family vault access.
  • Family vault data: Subject names, birth years, and optional notes you add about the people you are recording.
  • Questions: Text questions submitted by family members, including category and who submitted them.
  • Audio recordings: Voice recordings captured in-app and stored in your family's private vault. These are the core of the service.
  • Reactions: Text responses left by family members on individual recordings.
  • Photos: Optional photos attached to questions or recordings as memory triggers or context.
  • Activity data: Records of who added questions, recordings, and reactions within your family vault — visible only to members of that vault.
  • Crash reports: Anonymous crash data via Firebase Crashlytics to improve app stability.

Some of this information — including subject names, birth years, notes, photos, and the voices captured in recordings — relates to the people being recorded, who may not have accounts of their own. Section 10D describes how we treat information about recorded people who are not users.

3. What we never collect or create

  • Your location at any time
  • Voiceprints, biometric templates, or facial-recognition data — we do not analyze recordings or photos to identify or authenticate anyone
  • Messages outside the app's reaction feature
  • Social media profiles or third-party account data
  • Payment information (handled entirely by Apple)
  • Sensitive personal information such as Social Security numbers, government-issued ID, or financial account data

People you record (subjects) do not need accounts. We collect information about them only through what you and your vault members voluntarily enter and record. See Section 10D.

4. How we use your information

  • To operate the app: Store and deliver your family's recordings, questions, reactions, and photos securely within your family vault.
  • To improve the app: Anonymous, aggregate crash analytics only. No behavioral tracking, no individual-level analytics.
  • To communicate with you: Transactional emails only — password reset, account confirmation, and family invites you explicitly trigger. No marketing emails without your explicit consent.

5. We do not sell or share your personal information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information or your family's recordings with any third party for any commercial purpose. Ever.

Your recordings, questions, reactions, and photos are visible only to members of your family vault — people you explicitly invited or accepted an invitation from — unless a vault admin deliberately shares an individual recording outside the vault using the Share Recording feature described in Section 7A. Nothing is ever shared automatically.

6. Family vault isolation

Each family vault is completely isolated. Members can only access data from vaults they belong to. Row-level security policies enforce this at the database level — it is not possible for one family to access another family's data even with a valid account.

If you belong to multiple family vaults, each is a separate, isolated environment. Your activity in one vault is not visible to members of another.

7. Audio recordings — special protections

Audio recordings are the most sensitive data we store. We treat them accordingly.

  • All recordings are stored in private, access-controlled storage buckets.
  • Access URLs are signed and time-limited — they expire automatically and are not designed for access outside the app. The only exception is deliberate sharing by a vault admin through the Share Recording feature (Section 7A).
  • Recordings are encrypted on your device before upload (AES-256) and remain encrypted in storage, in addition to encrypted connections in transit. Encryption keys are managed within our infrastructure — this means we are technically capable of accessing recordings, and we will not do so except as described in this policy or as required by valid legal process. The Service is not a zero-knowledge system.
  • Recordings are not analyzed, transcribed, or used for advertising, profiling, or AI training purposes.
  • We do not create voiceprints or biometric templates from recordings, and we do not use recordings to identify or authenticate any person.
  • Recordings are not used for any commercial purpose beyond providing the playback, sharing, and export features you choose to use within the Service.

7A. Sharing recordings outside your vault

Remember Project Plus includes a Share Recording feature that lets a vault admin share a single recording with someone outside the vault. Sharing is always a deliberate action by a vault admin — nothing is ever shared automatically.

How sharing works:

  • Sharing creates a temporary copy of the recording that the recipient can play through a time-limited link. The shared copy expires and is automatically deleted after 72 hours or 3 plays, whichever comes first.
  • Before a share link is created, the sender must confirm they have the right and any necessary consent to share the recording outside the vault.
  • Before playback, the recipient must accept limited terms of use that prohibit redistribution, commercial use, voice cloning or synthetic-voice use, unlawful use, and attempts to bypass the time or play limits.
  • We keep a record of shares (who shared, when, and playback count) as part of your vault's activity data after the shared copy expires.

What we cannot control: once a recording has been played on someone else's device, we cannot prevent that person from capturing, re-recording, or preserving the audio by means outside the app. Time and play limits reduce this risk, but they do not eliminate it. Share only with people you trust.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Recordings, questions, reactions, photos: Retained for the life of your account unless you delete them individually or delete your account.
  • Account deletion: You may delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete My Data. Deletion removes your account, your submitted questions, your reactions, and any recordings you captured. Subjects you created and their associated recordings are also removed. This cannot be undone.
  • Expired shares: Temporary shared copies are deleted automatically when they expire (Section 7A). Share activity records are retained as part of vault activity data.
  • Exported copies: Exports downloaded by you or your family members exist outside our systems. Deleting content in the app does not delete copies that were previously exported or captured.
  • Requests from recorded people: See Section 10D for how a recorded person — or the parent or legal guardian of a recorded child — can request removal of recordings of them.
  • Export before deletion: We strongly recommend exporting your family's recordings before deleting your account. See Section 10.
  • After deletion: Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for a limited retention period before permanent deletion. Crash logs and anonymized technical records may be retained as required for legal compliance or system integrity.

8A. Health information and medical content

Remember Project is not a healthcare application and is not subject to HIPAA or other healthcare privacy regulations. We are not a covered entity under HIPAA, and our security practices are designed for family archiving — not healthcare data storage.

If you choose to record conversations that include discussion of health conditions, medical treatments, diagnoses, medications, or other sensitive health information, you do so at your own discretion and at your own risk. We have no control over the content of recordings and cannot prevent, detect, or restrict health-related content.

We strongly recommend:

  • Recording only with the full knowledge and consent of all people whose voices are captured
  • Being thoughtful about what sensitive health information is recorded and stored digitally
  • Understanding that even encrypted digital storage carries inherent privacy risks
  • Consulting a healthcare provider or privacy attorney if you have specific concerns about recording medical information

Remember Project applies the same security protections to all recordings regardless of content — but if you require HIPAA-compliant storage for medical records or protected health information, you should use a healthcare-specific service designed for that purpose.

10. Your right to export

Your recordings belong to you. At any time you may export your family vault as a ZIP file containing all audio recordings, named by question and subject — for example, Dad_What-did-the-kitchen-smell-like.m4a. This export is available from Settings → Export.

Remember Project Plus members may also generate a Memory Book — a formatted PDF of all recordings, questions, reactions, and photos, designed to be printed or shared with family.

We will never hold your data hostage. If you choose to leave the platform, your memories leave with you.

10A. Legal disclosures

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, subpoena, court order, or other valid legal process. We may also disclose information when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

Where legally permitted, we will attempt to notify you before disclosing your information in response to a legal request.

10B. Your responsibility to obtain consent

Recording laws vary by jurisdiction. Some locations require the consent of all parties being recorded before a recording may be made. Users are solely responsible for complying with applicable recording consent laws in their jurisdiction before using Remember Project to record others.

Remember Project does not monitor recording content or enforce consent requirements. By using the app to record another person, you represent that you have obtained any legally required consent.

Recordings that include a child: If a recording includes a child, you must be the child's parent or legal guardian, or have permission from the child's parent or legal guardian, before recording. The app may ask you to confirm this before recording begins.

The app may also ask you to affirmatively confirm — before recording or before sharing — that you have the legal right and any required consent. These confirmations may be logged as part of your vault's records.

10C. Vault administration and succession

Each family vault must have at least one designated admin. The admin controls who can access the vault, invite new members, and manage subjects and recordings.

We strongly recommend designating at least one co-admin for every family vault. If the sole admin becomes incapacitated, passes away, or loses access to their account, Remember Project will not unlock, transfer, or grant access to the vault at the request of family members, legal representatives, or estate executors, except pursuant to a valid court order or other binding legal process.

Remember Project is not a fiduciary and assumes no custodial responsibility for family vault data. We provide a secure tool for family archiving. We do not adjudicate disputes over vault ownership, inheritance, or access rights between family members, and we may require the agreement of authorized users or valid legal process before acting on any disputed request.

In the event that a vault becomes inaccessible due to loss of admin access:

  • Remember Project will not unlock or bypass vault security at the request of any person
  • Remember Project will not transfer vault ownership or data to any third party, including estate executors or legal representatives, except pursuant to a valid court order or other binding legal process
  • Vault data will remain stored but inaccessible until it is deleted per our standard data retention schedule or handled pursuant to a valid legal order

To protect your family's memories, we encourage all admins to designate a co-admin during vault setup and to keep their account credentials accessible to a trusted family member.

10D. People who are recorded but do not have accounts

Many of the people whose voices are preserved in Remember Project — parents, grandparents, and other family members — never create accounts. We take their privacy seriously.

  • What we store about them: their voices in recordings, and the names, birth years, notes, and photos that vault members voluntarily enter about them.
  • Who is responsible: the vault member who records or uploads content is responsible for having the recorded person's consent, and for parental or guardian permission where the recorded person is a child (Section 10B).
  • Removal requests: a recorded person — or the parent or legal guardian of a recorded child — may request removal of recordings of them by contacting support@rememberproject.app. We will take reasonable steps to verify the request, may notify the vault admin, and will remove the identified recordings from our active systems within 30 days of a verified request.

Removal cannot reach copies that were exported, shared, or captured outside the Service before the removal took effect.

11. In-app purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see, store, or handle your payment information.

12. Children's privacy

Remember Project is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow children under 13 to create accounts. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact support@rememberproject.app and we will delete the account promptly.

Children's voices may appear in recordings made by adult users — for example, a grandchild asking a question or answering one. The adult who makes the recording is responsible for having permission from the child's parent or legal guardian before recording (Section 10B). A parent or legal guardian may request removal of recordings of their child at any time (Section 10D).

13. Security

We use industry-standard security practices including:

  • On-device AES-256 encryption of audio recordings before upload, with service-managed keys (not zero-knowledge)
  • Encryption at rest for all database content and file storage
  • Encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS) for all data in transit
  • Row-level security policies scoped by family vault on all database tables
  • Signed, expiring URLs for all audio and photo file access
  • Security-definer database functions to prevent cross-family data exposure
  • Minimum data collection — we store only what is necessary to operate the service

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law.
